# Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Project Overview

This analysis examines supermarket transaction data to identify sales patterns, customer purchasing behavior, product category performance, and payment preferences. The findings can support data-driven decisions related to sales strategy, inventory planning, and customer engagement.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 322,966.75 |
| Total Transactions | 1,000 |
| Average Transaction Value | 322.97 |
| Total Gross Income | 15,379.37 |
| Average Customer Rating | Approximately 6.97 / 10 |

*Note: The dataset does not explicitly specify the currency, so sales figures are presented without a currency symbol.*

## 3. Key Business Findings

### 3.1 Product Category Performance

| Product Category | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Food and beverages | 56,144.84 |
| Sports and travel | 55,122.83 |
| Electronic accessories | 54,337.53 |
| Fashion accessories | 54,305.90 |
| Home and lifestyle | 53,861.91 |
| Health and beauty | 49,193.74 |

**Insight:** Food and beverages generated the highest sales, while health and beauty recorded the lowest sales among the six product categories.

**Recommendation:** Review category-level demand, stock availability, profit margins, and promotional performance before making inventory or marketing decisions.

### 3.2 City-Level Sales Performance

| City | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Naypyitaw | 110,568.71 |
| Yangon | 106,200.37 |
| Mandalay | 106,197.67 |

**Insight:** Naypyitaw recorded the highest total sales, while Yangon and Mandalay generated very similar sales.

**Recommendation:** Compare transaction volume, average transaction value, product mix, and customer preferences across cities to identify opportunities for targeted sales growth.

### 3.3 Customer Type Analysis

| Customer Type | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Member | 189,694.76 |
| Normal | 133,271.99 |

**Insight:** Member customers contributed more total sales than normal customers.

**Recommendation:** Evaluate membership benefits, repeat-purchase frequency, and customer retention to determine which loyalty initiatives are most effective.

### 3.4 Payment Preferences

| Payment Method | Transactions |
|---|---:|
| E-wallet | 345 |
| Cash | 344 |
| Credit card | 311 |

**Insight:** E-wallet payments were the most frequently used, closely followed by cash. Credit cards recorded the lowest transaction count.

**Recommendation:** Maintain convenient payment options across all methods and evaluate payment preferences when planning checkout improvements.

### 3.5 Customer Ratings

**Insight:** The overall average customer rating is approximately 6.97 out of 10. Ratings can help identify areas where the shopping experience may need improvement.

**Recommendation:** Compare ratings by product category, city, and customer type. Investigate lower-rated segments alongside customer feedback before implementing service improvements.

### 3.6 Monthly Sales Trends

The dataset covers January to March 2019.

**Analysis approach:** Compare monthly sales, transaction counts, and average transaction value to identify changes in performance over the period.

**Recommendation:** Use the monthly trend dashboard to investigate sales fluctuations and support future promotional and inventory planning. Avoid assuming seasonality from only three months of data.

## 4. Recommended Business Actions

1. **Improve category planning:** Investigate demand and margins across product categories to optimize inventory.
2. **Strengthen customer loyalty:** Assess member purchasing behavior and test retention initiatives.
3. **Improve local sales strategies:** Compare customer and product trends across the three cities.
4. **Support payment convenience:** Maintain a balanced range of payment methods based on transaction data.
5. **Monitor customer experience:** Use rating trends to prioritize areas for further investigation.
6. **Track performance regularly:** Monitor sales, transaction volume, average transaction value, and gross income in a dashboard.

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis identifies differences in category sales, city performance, customer-type contribution, and payment preferences. Combining SQL-based analysis with Power BI visualizations makes these patterns easier to interpret and provides a foundation for further business investigation.

**Tools used:** SQL (SQLite), Microsoft Power BI, Excel, and Markdown.
